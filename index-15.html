Privacy Policy

Ladybird High School
Last Updated: 1 October 2026

A.C.A.D.E.M.O.S. (“A.C.A.D.E.M.O.S.”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Ladybird High School mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose information when you use the App.

Ladybird High School is a school-management and educational application providing authorized students, parents/guardians, teachers, class masters, administrators, and other school members with access to school information and educational services.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and your role within the school, we may collect:

Account information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number, where provided
  • Password and authentication information
  • Account type and role
  • School affiliation
  • School-issued identification information
  • Access or voucher codes

School and academic information

  • Student identification number or matricule
  • Class and academic level
  • Academic year
  • Class master
  • Subjects
  • Timetables
  • Marks, grades, and examination results
  • Test and examination information
  • Attendance or other school records where enabled
  • Parent/guardian or school relationships

Educational activity

We may collect information about your use of educational features, including:

  • Courses and lessons accessed
  • Quiz and test attempts
  • Scores
  • Learning progress
  • Challenge participation
  • Achievements or XP
  • Educational resources accessed

Profile information

Users may provide:

  • Profile photograph
  • Name
  • Role
  • Class
  • School-related information

Communications and uploaded content

If communication or file-sharing features are available, we may process:

  • Messages
  • Images
  • Documents
  • Other attachments
  • Group memberships
  • Information required to deliver communications

Technical information

The App and its supporting services may automatically process technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating-system version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Network information
  • Crash and error information
  • Performance information
  • Authentication and session information
  • Technical identifiers required for security or service operation

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Connect users with Ladybird High School
  • Provide school-management services
  • Provide courses, quizzes, tests, and educational resources
  • Display academic information and timetables
  • Facilitate authorized school communication
  • Track educational progress
  • Maintain and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Protect accounts and systems
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access
  • Send necessary service notifications
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell student academic records or school records.

3. Academia

Ladybird High School may use educational content and services provided through the Academia platform.

Academia may provide general educational resources such as courses, books, quizzes, tests, and other learning materials. Ladybird High School may maintain school-specific information separately from general Academia content.

Access to school-specific information is controlled according to the user's role and authorization.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

The school

Relevant information may be made available to authorized Ladybird High School administrators, teachers, class masters, and other authorized personnel for legitimate educational and administrative purposes.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for services such as:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Databases
  • Authentication
  • File storage
  • Push notifications
  • Security
  • Crash reporting
  • Technical infrastructure

These providers process information as necessary to provide their services.

Legal and safety purposes

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law
  • Respond to lawful legal requests
  • Protect users or the public
  • Protect our rights or property
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents

5. Children's Privacy

Ladybird High School is an educational application and may be used by students under the age of 18.

We take the privacy of students and children seriously. We collect and process student information only for legitimate educational, school-administration, communication, security, and service purposes.

We do not sell children's personal information.

Where appropriate, school administrators, parents, or legal guardians may have rights concerning a student's information, subject to applicable law and appropriate verification.

The App does not intentionally use children's personal information for behavioral advertising.

6. Photos, Files, and Media

The App may request access to photos, images, documents, or other files when a user chooses to use a feature requiring such content.

For example, a user may voluntarily upload a profile photograph or attach a document or image to a communication.

We do not intentionally access a user's private files or media merely for the purpose of collecting them.

7. Camera

If a feature requires the camera, the App may request camera permission.

Camera access is used only for the feature requiring it, such as capturing a photograph or other user-initiated content.

If the current version of the App does not require camera functionality, camera access will not be requested.

8. Location Information

Ladybird High School does not intentionally collect precise device location unless a future feature specifically requires it and the relevant permission and disclosure are provided.

The App is not designed to continuously track users' physical locations.

9. Notifications

The App may send notifications concerning:

  • School announcements
  • Messages
  • Academic information
  • Tests and examinations
  • Courses and educational activity
  • Account and security matters
  • Other important service information

Users can control notification permissions through their device settings.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • Encryption during transmission
  • Authentication controls
  • Role-based access controls
  • Restricted administrative access
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Security monitoring
  • Access controls and logging
  • Backup and recovery procedures

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. We therefore cannot guarantee that information will always remain completely secure.

11. Access Controls

Access to information is based on the user's role and authorization.

For example, students, teachers, parents, class masters, and administrators may receive different levels of access.

Users are not automatically granted access to information belonging to other users merely because they use the App.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the App and its services
  • Maintain legitimate school and educational records
  • Maintain account functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Maintain security
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

When information is no longer required for a legitimate purpose, we will delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and school-record requirements.
